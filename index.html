Hidden meanings

"The moon is beautiful, isn't it?" is a poetic, indirect way of saying "I love you" in Japanese (Tsuki ga kirei desu ne, 月が綺麗ですね).

Hiraeth is a Welsh word that describes a deep, soulful longing for a home, time, or place to which you cannot return-or one that perhaps never even existed.
